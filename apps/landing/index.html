Hear a cloned voice before it costs you.

Doppelgänger listens to the other side of a browser call and warns you when the voice sounds synthetic. It runs in your browser, and no audio leaves your device.

Try the live demo Watch the 3-minute video

One call, three moments

  1. The call

    Your finance director calls on a video meeting. It sounds exactly like her, and she needs an urgent transfer before the bank closes.

  2. The alert

    The calm interface starts to rot. After several seconds of sustained evidence it reads: Doppelgänger detected. Do not approve anything on this call.

  3. The protocol

    Ask the code word you agreed on. Hang up and call her back on a number you already know. Report the call.

Check it yourself: nothing leaves your device

The model and the audio both stay in your browser. The page downloads the app, the model file and its runtime from this site, and then sends nothing to any server.

  1. Open the live demo and press F12 to open your browser's developer tools on the Network tab.
  2. Start a replay or a capture and watch the requests while it listens.
  3. Confirm that every request goes to this site and only downloads files. Nothing is sent back while it listens; a replay clip is downloaded, never uploaded.

The rules behind this are in the security steering and the model card.

Run it your way

Tier What you need What runs where Which model
Demo A Chromium browser Everything in your browser, served from this site Light model in the browser
Self-hosted Python 3 and the installer The same app, served from your own machine on 127.0.0.1 Light model in the browser
Pro engine Docker with Compose v2 and the installer A local container, reachable only from your machine Heavier model in the engine, plus file Autopsy

On Linux or WSL2, from a clone of the repository:

git clone https://github.com/brennocm/doppelganger.git
cd doppelganger
scripts/install.sh --static
scripts/install.sh

The first command serves the app on your machine. The second starts the Pro engine. macOS is untested.

Limits

  • The dissonance score is a probabilistic estimate that a voice is synthetic. It is not proof.
  • It does not identify people. It cannot tell you who is speaking.
  • Call codecs, background noise, accents, short replies and voice generators the model has never heard all cause mistakes, in both directions.
  • Alerts wait for sustained evidence, so a short clip may never reach one.

The model card has the details.
